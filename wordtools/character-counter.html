Why character count matters more than word count sometimes

Most platforms and forms don't actually limit how many words you write — they limit how many characters. A short word like "a" and a long one like "characteristically" count the same toward a word limit, but very differently toward a character limit. That's why Twitter/X, SMS, meta descriptions and bio fields all cap characters, not words.

How this tool counts characters

The character count includes every letter, number, space and punctuation mark. The "characters without spaces" figure removes whitespace, which is useful when a platform's limit is documented as counting only visible characters.

Frequently asked questions

Does SMS really limit messages to 160 characters?
Yes — a single SMS segment is limited to 160 characters (or 70 for messages containing non-GSM characters like emoji). Longer messages are split into multiple segments by carriers.
Why does Google sometimes show a shorter title than I wrote?
Google truncates title tags and meta descriptions based on pixel width, not a strict character count, so actual cutoff varies by the letters used. The limits shown here are commonly used safe estimates.
