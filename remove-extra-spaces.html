Why pasted text gets messy

Copying text out of a PDF, an old Word document, or a spreadsheet cell often brings along extra spaces between words, stray tabs, or a blank line after every sentence. That hidden whitespace can break formatting when you paste the text into a CMS, an email, or code.

What each button does

  • Remove extra spaces: collapses repeated spaces or tabs within each line down to a single space, and trims leading/trailing space from each line.
  • Remove line breaks: joins everything into a single paragraph, replacing line breaks with a single space — useful when a PDF has broken one sentence across several lines.
  • Remove blank lines: deletes empty lines while keeping your paragraph breaks and line order intact.
  • Trim each line: removes leading and trailing whitespace from every line without otherwise changing the text.

Frequently asked questions

Will this change the words in my text?
No — only whitespace (spaces, tabs, blank lines) is affected. Letters, numbers and punctuation are left exactly as you wrote them.
Is my text uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything runs locally in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.
