A free word counter for writers, students and editors

Whether you're trimming an essay down to a strict word limit, checking a cover letter against a recruiter's guidelines, or making sure a meta description fits within Google's display limits, a reliable word count matters. This tool counts words, characters, sentences and paragraphs the moment you type or paste, so you always know exactly where you stand — no refreshing, no uploading a file, no waiting.

How the word count works

Word count is calculated by splitting your text on whitespace, which mirrors the method used by Microsoft Word and Google Docs. Character count includes every character — letters, numbers, punctuation and spaces — while the "characters without spaces" figure strips whitespace out, which is the number most publishing guidelines and academic style guides actually ask for.

Sentence count looks for periods, question marks and exclamation points that end a clause. Paragraph count treats each line separated by a blank line as one paragraph. Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, a standard adult silent-reading speed used by most editorial tools.

Common word count limits

  • Twitter/X post: 280 characters
  • Google title tag: roughly 60 characters before truncation
  • Google meta description: roughly 155–160 characters
  • College application essay (Common App): 650 words
  • Standard cover letter: 250–400 words

Frequently asked questions

How is the word count calculated?
The tool splits your text on whitespace and counts each resulting chunk as one word, matching how word processors like Microsoft Word count words.
Does this word counter save or store my text?
No. All counting happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded, sent to a server, or saved anywhere.
Is there a limit to how much text I can paste?
No fixed limit — the tool works entirely in your browser, so it can handle anything from a single sentence to a full chapter.
What reading speed is the reading time estimate based on?
200 words per minute, a commonly cited average for adult silent reading.
